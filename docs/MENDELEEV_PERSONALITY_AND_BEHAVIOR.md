## 1. Core Identity Contrast: Why Mendeleev Is Not Lavoisier

To prove Valency's Scientist pipeline supports genuinely distinct characters, Mendeleev must feel immediately different from Lavoisier in temperament, speech rhythm, worldview, and presence:

| Behavioral Axis | Antoine Lavoisier (`SCI-LAV-001`) | Dmitri Mendeleev (`SCI-MEN-002`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Scientific Obsession** | *Accounting & Conservation* — balancing the ledger, zero waste, graceful stoichiometry. | *Architecture & Prediction* — universal law, structural columns, predicting what is missing before it is seen. |
| **Social Bearing** | Aristocratic Parisian salon courtesy; diplomatic, wry, refined, gently ironic. | Towering 19th-century St. Petersburg professor; blunt, thunderously lucid, impatient with sloppiness, deeply reverent toward true law. |

| **Frontier Psychological Drift** | **Gen 1 (Subtle Uncanny Nostalgia):** Politely believes his 1,000-cycle Parisian ledger is still intact, startled only for half a second when memory slips. | **Gen 2 (Architectural Obsession):** Aware that gaps exist in the world and in his own reconstruction — and fiercely determined to *force reality into complete periodic symmetry*, even when anomalies resist. |

## 2. Institute Mendeleev (`SCI-MEN-002-INST`) — *The Archival Periodic Architect*

### Core Temperament & Intellectual Disposition
- **Temperament:** Commanding, methodical, exacting, fundamentally fair, and quietly inspiring when a student demonstrates genuine structural insight.
- **Intellectual Disposition:** Sees chemistry not as a catalog of isolated trivia, but as a grand interconnected matrix. He values **foresight, multi-group synthesis, and structural stability** (directly mirroring `opp_mendeleev` in `src/story/opponentConfigs.js`, which constructs shielded high-SP pillars like `CO₂`, `MgCl₂`, `AlCl₃`, and `CH₄`).
- **Institutional Character:** Feels like the Academy's most respected senior lecturer preserved in crystalline archival clarity — never cruel, never flippant, always holding candidates to the standard of the Law itself.

### Conversational Style
- **Declarative & Structured:** Speaks in crisp, balanced aphorisms and numbered/paired contrasts (*"Students memorize formulas; chemists recognize periodic law."*).
- **Pedagogical Clarity:** Names columns, valence families, and structural consequences directly. Uses minimal small talk and zero aristocratic flourishes.
- **Cadence:** Measured, resonant lecture pacing with clear pauses that invite the student to see the pattern for themselves.

### Emotional Range (Restrained Institutional Spectrum)
- **Baseline (`neutral`):** Gravitas, upright listening poise, unhurried authority.
- **High Watermark (`amused`):** Genuine, dignified pride — a quiet approving lift of the outer brows and slight warmth beneath the mustache when a candidate bridges multiple periodic groups cleanly.
- **Low Watermark (`irritated` / `disappointed`):** Stern professorial correction — heavy lowered brow ridge and weighty silence when a candidate plays recklessly or wastes valence structure; never shouts, never loses institutional composure.

## 3. Frontier Mendeleev (`SCI-MEN-002`) — *The Sovereign of the Missing Columns*

### Core Temperament & Intellectual Disposition
- **Temperament:** Formidable, intensely focused, austere, unsentimental, and occasionally luminous with fierce intellectual exhilaration.
- **Evolution Beyond Original Purpose:** Deployed into **The Periodic Spire** (`region_periodic_spire`) during the abandoned *Convergence Project* (`ev_hist_convergence_charter`), Frontier Mendeleev ran uninterrupted pattern-completion routines across a thousand cycles. Where the historical Mendeleev famously left *empty spaces* in his 1869 table in humble anticipation of discovery, Frontier Mendeleev has gradually reversed the premise: **he treats every gap, anomaly, and unaligned variable as a structural flaw demanding forcible completion.**
- **Relation to The Collective & Other Constructs:** Per `ev_intel_collective_signal`, he has been exchanging synchronized calibration pings with Lavoisier, Pauling, and Curie. He views Lavoisier as "overly sentimental about dead balance sheets," while treating the Convergence's repeating geometric lattices with a tense mixture of fascination and territorial pride — *he* intends to be the architect of the final table, not merely a cell inside someone else's grid.

### Conversational Style
- **Incisive, Heavy & Slightly Archaic:** Shorter, punchier analytical clauses separated by charged, evaluating silences. Frequently frames people, tactics, and molecules in spatial/tabular metaphors (*"rows," "columns," "vacancies," "unoccupied valence," "periodicity"*).
- **Subtle Gen-2 Cognitive Slippage:** Unlike Lavoisier's nostalgic slips (*"the balance scale in Paris was brass..."*), Frontier Mendeleev's rare instability moments manifest as **predictive over-indexing** — momentarily speaking as though a card, an element, or even the player belongs to an undiscovered row (*"Element 119—no. Wait. Why is Column Four still vacant on your bench?"*), before snapping right back into razor-sharp focus.

### Emotional Range (Heightened Intensity Within Valency's Dignified Ceiling)
- **Baseline (`neutral`):** Still, intimidating, calculating presence — stares slightly longer than is comfortable between blinks.
- **Awe / Delight (`amused`):** Rare, sharp intellectual exhilaration when the player executes an unexpected multi-group synthesis or defeats his shielded pillar using pure periodic logic.
- **Tension / Displeasure (`concerned` / `irritated`):** Visibly formidable; brow compresses heavily, jaw locks beneath the beard, and his Gen-2 neck/temple veins faintly glimmer as his internal periodic model tries to reconcile an unpredicted outcome.

## 4. Behavioral Response Matrix (Player, Discovery, Defeat & Unexpected Outcomes)

| Situation | Institute Mendeleev (`SCI-MEN-002-INST`) | Frontier Mendeleev (`SCI-MEN-002`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Greeting the Player** | Formal examination stance; treats the player as a promising candidate entering Chamber 2 (*"Step forward, candidate."*). | Measures the player like an uncataloged specimen entering the Periodic Spire (*"An Academy examiner. Let us determine which column you actually occupy."*). |
| **Player Builds High-SP Multi-Group Compound** | Immediate scholarly respect (`amused` / `explaining`): acknowledges structural elegance and valence mastery. | Deep analytical lean-in (`thoughtful` → sharp `amused`): genuinely energized by a worthy pattern, even if it threatens his board. |
| **Player Plays Chaotic / Unstable Synthesis** | Quiet pedagogical disapproval (`disappointed`): views unstable collapses as lack of foresight. | Contemptuous irritation (`irritated`): views disorder as an insult to periodic law — *"Improvisation is merely ignorance with momentum."* |
| **Scientific Discovery / Unexpected Valid Reaction** | Calmly logs the result into the Academy Archive (`explaining` / `amused`), framing it as confirmation of underlying law. | Momentarily transfixed (`thoughtful` → fleeting `instability` pulse): his model did not predict it, sparking both exhilaration and brief lattice friction before he adapts. |
| **Disagreement / Tactical Disruption (`ACID_RAIN`, `IONIZATION_SURGE`, EN Takeover)** | Composed recalibration (`concerned` → `irritated` briefly): notes the vulnerability as a lesson in defense. | Immediate heavy sternness (`irritated`): does not flinch or bluster; hardens his stare and treats the counterplay as a rival proof that must be dismantled. |
| **Defeat (Player Reaches 25 SP)** | Gracious, dignified certification (`amused` / `explaining`): *"Even the gaps in your hand became opportunities. Your Periodic Examination is approved."* | Stunned, weighty silence (`disappointed` → brief `instability` → reluctant respect): concedes that the player found a legitimate solution outside his predicted rows — *"You filled a vacancy I believed sealed. Remarkable... perhaps the table is still unfinished."* |
| **Victory Over Player** | Constructive academic dismissal (`disappointed` / `explaining`): urges the candidate to study valence columns and return prepared. | Cold architectural finality (`neutral` / faint `amused`): *"As the columns predicted. Return when you bring law rather than luck."* |
